# Claude OS

Claude OS is my personal control surface for everything I run through Claude Code:
a [[Projects|portfolio]] of apps, the [[Agents|agents]] and [[Skills|skills]] I
delegate to, the [[Plugins|plugins]] and [[MCP-Connectors|connectors]] wired in,
and the [[Hosting|infrastructure]] that ships it.

It is rendered by **BrainTree OS** (a local-first brain viewer) and published as a
read-only mirror at [[Domains|BrainOs.postiusgroup.com]].

## Layers
1. **Build** — [[Projects]] (what ships)
2. **Delegate** — [[Agents]] + [[Skills]]
3. **Connect** — [[Plugins]] + [[MCP-Connectors]]
4. **Run** — [[Hosting]] + [[Deployment]] + [[Domains]]
